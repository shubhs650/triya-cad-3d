- 2026-10-18 — Nozzle geometry (T3.3): neck, pad and bore are joined into the host body by `solid.cylinder join/cut` and `solid.combine`; a rod's inner end is a plane inside the wall where one fits, else it is trimmed by the host's mid-wall surface (cylinder, or the crown sphere of a torispherical head). Kernel trials (18 Oct): rod join + bore is accurate (+0.4 %, −0.02 %) and takes 5–30 s; cutting a hole at the neck OD and then joining a neck of the same OD failed after 207 s, and a cylinder-in-cylinder intersect for the pad failed, so neither is used. `set_in` and `set_on` build the same solid (welds differ only on the drawing).
- 2026-10-18 — Nozzle validation: the host's volume is measured before and after on a scratch session; the change must match `nozzle::expected_change` (a polar quadrature through the exact surfaces) within 2 % of the change + 3e-4 of the host volume (the tessellated measure's error on a large body). The second term means a small nozzle on a large course is only checked for gross failures; recorded so T3.5 can tighten it if needed.
- 2026-10-18 — Pads only on cylinders in the beta (on a head: warning, drawn on the sheet). An ellipsoidal head whose dish is too curved under a wide neck for a plane in the wall gets the neck square from the deepest inside point (an inside projection, with a warning) instead of a revolved-cavity trim, which did not finish in 10 min in a trial.
- 2026-10-18 — `projection.from = cl` on head frames is measured from the head's tangent-line plane; `axial` is ignored for head frames; tilted nozzles turn about the tangent through the outside-surface point at the elevation.

- 2026-10-18 — Fallback (T3.5): separate bodies, not a component, because regenerate's cleanup tracks only its own timeline features; the separate neck has no pad (the pad is on the sheet). PLAN's degenerate case (equal-radius crossing cylinders) joins correctly in this kernel (6 in neck on 6 in pipe: +476,885 mm³ vs 473,544 expected), so the AT uses a pad wider than its shell. The flange is built after the neck so a bolt-hole failure (N5 on vessel A: the patterned cut "came out empty") falls back to one cut per hole, then to the plain ring. The Phase 3 exit test (~16 min) is `#[ignore]`d in the crates job and run with `--ignored` in its own CI job.
- 2026-10-18 — Nozzle flanges are separate bodies, not joined to the neck: a flange is its own part on the BOM and drawing, and a coaxial join adds a coplanar-face boolean for nothing. The gasket face is the projection's end (RF top / FF).
